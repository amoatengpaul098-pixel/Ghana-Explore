ROOTED IN HERITAGE

A culture rich in meaning.

Ghana’s cultural life is diverse, shaped by many peoples, languages, histories and creative traditions.

01

Kente & textiles

Kente weaving is especially associated with Asante and Ewe communities. Patterns and colours can carry meanings and are used for ceremonies and important occasions.

02

Festivals & community

Traditional festivals bring people together for remembrance, thanksgiving, renewal and the celebration of community identity. Dates vary by community and year.

03

Music & dance

From highlife and hiplife to traditional drumming and dance, Ghana’s soundscape reflects local roots and global influences.

04

Languages

English is Ghana’s official language. Akan varieties, Ewe, Ga, Dagbani, Dagaare and many other Ghanaian languages are spoken across the country.
