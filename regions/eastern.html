ABOUT THE REGION

Eastern Region

The Eastern Region of Ghana is known for its lush landscapes, including mountains, waterfalls, and forest reserves. It is home to the Aburi Botanical Gardens and offers a variety of outdoor activities for nature enthusiasts.

REGIONAL HIGHLIGHTS

  • Aburi Botanical Gardens
  • Boti Falls
  • Afram Plains
  • Mount Afadja
  • Koforidua Market
