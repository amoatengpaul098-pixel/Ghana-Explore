Explore the character of:: Bono.

Discover the character of the Bono region, a lush and welcoming area in Ghana known for its vibrant towns, agricultural communities, and rich cultural heritage.

REGIONAL HIGHLIGHTS

What to see and do in:: Bono.

  • Visit the Sunyani Central Market for local crafts and produce.
  • Explore the Kintampo Waterfalls and surrounding natural beauty.
  • Experience traditional festivals and cultural events in local towns.
  • Discover historical sites and learn about the region's heritage.
